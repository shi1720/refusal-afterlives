Shivam Gupta is an independent researcher and applied AI engineer with a background in computer science and design at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi. His work connects software systems, human-centered design, and the evaluation of interactive technologies. He has led engineering and product development across commercial applications and founded a software studio. His current research examines how computational representations shape the choices offered to people, with particular attention to the relationship between interface language and executable behavior. For IASPR, he brings reproducible source analysis into conversation with popular romance scholarship on agency, intimate choice, and hopeful narrative futures.
